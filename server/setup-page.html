Altbot server setup

Puts your fake-money trading bot on a free Oracle Cloud server, running 24/7. You check on it from your phone. Takes about 20 minutes.

STEP 1Get the Tailscale app

Tailscale makes a private link between your phone and the server, so only you can open the bot.

  1. Install Tailscale from the Play Store and sign in (Google login is fine).
  2. In your browser, open login.tailscale.com/admin/settings/keys
  3. Tap Generate auth key, leave the settings as they are, tap Generate key, and copy it. It starts with tskey-auth-

STEP 2Build your setup script

Everything happens on this page. Your key and password are never sent anywhere.

STEP 3Create the server on Oracle

  1. Log in at cloud.oracle.com, tap the menu ☰ and go to Compute → Instances, then tap Create instance.
  2. Name: altbot
  3. Image: tap Change image and choose Ubuntu (24.04).
  4. Shape: tap Change shape → Ampere → VM.Standard.A1.Flex, set 1 OCPU and 6 GB memory. It shows "Always Free eligible".
  5. SSH keys: leave Generate a key pair for me selected and tap Save private key (a spare way in, just in case).
  6. Open Show advanced options → Management → Paste cloud-init script, and paste your setup script from Step 2.
  7. Tap Create.

If it says "Out of capacity" for Ampere, choose shape VM.Standard.E2.1.Micro instead. It's also free.

STEP 4Open your bot

  1. Wait about 10 minutes while the server sets itself up.
  2. Make sure the Tailscale app is on on your phone.
  3. Open http://altbot:8080 in your phone's browser.
  4. Log in with trader and your password.
